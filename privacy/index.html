1. Core principle

Lyhio is designed to process as much information as reasonably possible on your device.

Lyhio does not require you to create a Lyhio account or public profile.

Most creation and viewing operations take place locally on your device. Certain features use Apple services or direct device-to-device communication where necessary to provide the feature you choose.

Lyhio does not sell personal data and does not use your content for advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. Photos and content you choose

Lyhio allows you to select photos from sources made available by iOS and use them to create Scratches or Mosaics.

Depending on the feature you use, Lyhio may process:

  • the image you selected;
  • crop, zoom, position, or layout information;
  • the selected Scratch style;
  • optional text or note you add;
  • temporary identifiers required to create or deliver the item;
  • creation time and technical state required for the feature.

Lyhio processes this information only to provide the feature you requested.

Lyhio does not scan your entire photo library. Access is limited by the permissions and selections provided through iOS.

3. Scratch and Apple Messages

When you send a Scratch through Apple Messages, Lyhio uses Apple technologies including Messages and CloudKit to make the selected Scratch available to the recipient.

For this feature, Lyhio may upload to Apple CloudKit:

  • the selected Scratch image (as a CloudKit asset);
  • the selected Scratch style identifier;
  • optional note text, when provided;
  • a randomly generated message UUID used as the record name;
  • format version and creation / expiry timestamps.

The Apple Messages payload itself contains lightweight technical information (including the message UUID) used to identify and retrieve the Scratch. Lyhio does not operate a separate developer-owned server for this transport.

Scratch content is stored in an Apple CloudKit public database used by Lyhio for delivery — not in a private or shared CloudKit database. The app retrieves a record by its message UUID rather than presenting a browsable gallery of other users' Scratches. Practical confidentiality therefore depends on the UUID remaining unguessable and on Apple CloudKit platform controls; it is not the same as private per-user CloudKit storage.

Scratch delivery and retention

Scratch content used for iMessage delivery is stored temporarily in Apple CloudKit.

An unopened Scratch is generally available for retrieval for approximately 14 days from the time it is uploaded.

CloudKit cleanup is performed on a best-effort basis. Deletion at an exact time is not guaranteed, because cleanup is client-triggered and CloudKit does not provide a native per-record TTL for this implementation.

The 14-day period applies to the temporary CloudKit delivery copy. It does not impose a 14-day expiration on a Scratch that has already been successfully obtained by the recipient's device.

Apple's processing of iMessage, iCloud, and CloudKit is also governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Receiving and revealing Scratches

When you open a compatible Lyhio Scratch received through Apple Messages, the Lyhio app may retrieve the corresponding image, style identifier, and optional note from Apple CloudKit using the message UUID.

The Scratch is then rendered and revealed on your device.

When a recipient successfully opens a Scratch, Lyhio downloads the content from CloudKit and may store a local copy on that recipient's device so that the Scratch can be reopened without downloading the original CloudKit record again. The local copy may be created as soon as the content is successfully fetched.

A locally stored Scratch is not a guaranteed permanent archive. It may become unavailable if Lyhio is deleted, local app data is removed, the device or its storage is lost or reset, or for other device or operating-system reasons. Lyhio does not promise indefinite local storage.

Lyhio does not use received Scratch content for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

Recipients should understand that content sent to another person can be viewed, captured, copied, photographed, or otherwise retained by that recipient using device or system features outside Lyhio's control.

5. Mosaic and nearby device communication

Mosaic allows nearby compatible devices to participate in a shared image-reveal experience.

Lyhio uses local or peer-to-peer Apple networking technologies to discover and communicate with nearby participating devices.

Depending on the Mosaic session, participating devices may exchange:

  • temporary device and session identifiers;
  • connection state;
  • Mosaic participation information;
  • the image fragment assigned to a participating device;
  • optional note or metadata required for the Mosaic;
  • session control messages.

Mosaic communication is intended to occur directly between participating nearby devices and is not routed through a Lyhio-operated server.

Lyhio does not request GPS coordinates for Mosaic.

Nearby device discovery may rely on Apple networking technologies and system permissions such as Local Network access.

6. Local data and Last Creation

Lyhio may store app settings and limited creation state locally on your device.

If Show Last Creation is enabled, Lyhio may locally retain your most recent Scratch or Mosaic so it can be displayed again on the Home screen and reused.

Only the latest supported creation is retained for this feature. A newer creation replaces the previous one according to the app's current behavior.

If you disable Show Last Creation, Lyhio removes the locally stored Last Creation data used by this feature and falls back to the bundled demo content.

Local information may also be removed when you delete the app or when iOS removes application data.

Lyhio may receive information from StoreKit that is required to determine whether Plus features are available, including:

Lyhio does not receive your full payment card number, security code, or complete payment credentials.

Payments and subscription management are handled by Apple.

Restore Purchases may request Apple to synchronize eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account.

11. Data retention

Local information

Settings and locally retained app state remain on your device until they are replaced, deleted through an app feature, removed when you uninstall the app, or otherwise cleared by iOS.

Scratch CloudKit content and local copies

Scratch CloudKit delivery records are generally intended to remain available for approximately 14 days from upload. Cleanup is best-effort and client-triggered as described in section 3.

Unopened Scratch content that was never successfully obtained on a recipient device may become unavailable when the CloudKit record is no longer present.

A Scratch that has already been successfully obtained may remain available from a local copy on that recipient device. The app does not currently enforce a 14-day expiration on that local copy, and Lyhio does not promise indefinite local retention.

Support requests

Support emails and related technical information may be retained for up to 24 months after a request is closed, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary for legal obligations, dispute resolution, fraud or abuse prevention, or establishment and defence of legal claims.

We aim not to retain personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.
