3. Scratches and Apple Messages

Lyhio allows compatible Scratches to be sent through Apple Messages.

Scratch delivery may use Apple technologies including Messages, iCloud, and CloudKit.

The real Scratch image and optional note are not necessarily contained directly in the Apple Messages payload. Lyhio may use a temporary identifier to retrieve the corresponding Scratch content through Apple CloudKit.

Scratch availability

An unopened Scratch is generally intended to remain available for retrieval for approximately 14 days from upload.

The 14-day period is a delivery and first-open availability window. It is not a promise that CloudKit content will be deleted at an exact time.

Cleanup of temporary CloudKit delivery records is performed on a best-effort basis and may depend on the sending device subsequently running Lyhio. As a result, exact deletion timing cannot be guaranteed.

If an unopened Scratch is no longer available from CloudKit and the recipient's device does not already have a usable local copy, the Scratch may no longer be opened.

4. Received Scratches and local copies

When a recipient successfully opens a Scratch, Lyhio may store a local copy of the Scratch on that recipient's device.

A Scratch that has already been successfully obtained may therefore remain available on that device even after the temporary CloudKit delivery copy is no longer available.

The App does not currently impose the 14-day delivery expiration on a Scratch that has already been successfully obtained and retained locally.

However, local storage is not a permanent archive. A locally stored Scratch may become unavailable if the App is deleted, application data is removed, the device is reset, storage is lost, or because of other device or operating-system behavior.

Lyhio does not guarantee permanent or indefinite availability of any Scratch.

Content sent to another person may also be captured, copied, photographed, screenshotted, or otherwise retained using system or device features outside Lyhio's control.
